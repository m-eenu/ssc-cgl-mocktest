Part 1: Top 25 English Grammar Rules

Rule 1: Possessive Adjectives vs Pronouns

  • Possessive adjectives come before a noun, while possessive pronouns stand alone.
  • **Adjectives:** My, Our, Your, His/Her/Its, Their.
  • **Pronouns:** Mine, Ours, Yours, His/Hers, Theirs.

Rules 6, 7 & 8: Pronoun Agreements

  • **Rule 6:** Relative pronoun verb agrees with its antecedent.
  • **Rule 7:** When subject is 'one', use **one's** instead of *his*.
  • **Rule 8:** With *all, some, most, half + of*, verb agrees with the following noun.
