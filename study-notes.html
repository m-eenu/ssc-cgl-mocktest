3. All Important Battles of Indian History (Complete Timeline)

Ancient India Battles

  • c. 14th cent. BCE: Battle of Ten Kings — Bharat vs 10 kings (Bharat's victory; Mentioned in Rig Veda)
  • 326 BCE: Battle of Hydaspes — Alexander vs Porus (Porus won; Stopped Alexander's advance)
  • 305-303 BCE: Seleucid-Mauryan War — Seleucus Nicator vs Chandragupta Maurya (Maurya's victory; Treaty, 500 elephants)
  • 261 BCE: Kalinga War — Ashoka vs Kalinga (Ashoka's victory; Turned Ashoka to Buddhism)
  • 618 CE: Narmada Conflict — Harsha vs Pulakeshin II (Pulakeshin II won; Check on Harsha's expansion)
  • 618-619 CE: Pullalur Battle — Pallava vs Chalukya (Pallava victory; Chalukya advance stopped)
  • 642 CE: Vatapi Battle — Pulakeshin II vs Narasimhavarman I (Pallava victory; Chalukya capital captured)

Medieval India Battles

  • 1191: First Tarain — Prithviraj Chauhan vs Muhammad Ghori (Chauhan victory; End of Ghori's first invasion)
  • 1192: Second Tarain — Prithviraj Chauhan vs Muhammad Ghori (Ghori victory; End of Rajput dominance)
  • 1526: First Panipat — Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi (Babur victory; Foundation of Mughal Empire)
  • 1527: Khanwa — Babur vs Rana Sanga (Babur victory; Rana Sanga defeated)
  • 1528: Chanderi — Babur vs Medini Rai (Babur victory; Chanderi captured)
  • 1529: Ghaghra — Humayun vs Afghan chiefs (Mughal victory; Afghans defeated)
  • 1556: Second Panipat — Akbar vs Hemu (Akbar victory; End of Hemu's rule)
  • 1565: Talikota — Vijayanagara vs Deccan Sultanates (Deccan Sultanates victory; End of Vijayanagara Empire)
  • 1576: Haldighati — Maharana Pratap vs Akbar (Mughal victory; Pratap retreated, resistance continued)
  • 1761: Third Panipat — Marathas vs Ahmad Shah Abdali (Maratha defeat; End of Maratha dominance)

Modern India Battles

  • 1739: Karnal — Maratha vs Nadir Shah (Maratha defeat; End of Mughal power)
  • 1741: Colachel — Maratha vs British (British victory; End of Maratha naval power)
  • 1757: Plassey — Siraj-ud-Daulah vs British (British victory; British control in Bengal)
  • 1760: Wandiwash — French vs British (British victory; End of French power)
  • 1764: Buxar — Mughal + Awadh vs British (British victory; Diwani rights to East India Co.)
  • 1767-1799: Anglo-Mysore Wars — Mysore vs British (British victory; End of Tipu Sultan's power)
  • 1775-1818: Anglo-Maratha Wars — Marathas vs British (British victory; End of Maratha power)
  • 1845-1849: Anglo-Sikh Wars — Sikh Empire vs British (British victory; Punjab annexed)
  • 1839-1842, 1878-1880: Anglo-Afghan Wars — British vs Afghan (British failure; End of British control in Afghanistan)
  • 1824-1826, 1852, 1885: Anglo-Burmese Wars — British vs Burma (British victory; Burma annexed)
  • 1944: Imphal — Allies vs Japanese (Allied victory; Turning point in WWII / INA)
